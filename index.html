Why Families Choose Us

Free Government Education

Tamil-medium instruction for Classes 6–12, run under the Tamil Nadu School Education Department at no cost to families.

Hi-Tech Computer Lab

33 computers across campus, including a 21-system Hi-Tech Lab with internet, firewall protection, and an AI-enabled system.

Mid-Day Meal Scheme

Nutritious meals provided daily under the state Mid-Day Meal Scheme, with a dedicated kitchen facility.

Accessible Campus

17 of our 28 classrooms are accessible to differently-abled students, with 2 dedicated accessible restrooms.
