Facilities

What the school campus offers students.

📖

Library

A school library with a collection of over 2,670 books covering academic and general reading material.

💻

Hi-Tech Computer Lab

33 computers across the school, including a 21-system (20+1) Hi-Tech Lab with working internet, a firewall system, and 1 AI-enabled system. Report storage facility available in the lab.

🏫

Classrooms

28 classrooms across the campus serving Classes 6 through 12, including 4 fitted with smart boards.

🤾

Playground

An outdoor playground for sports and physical education.

♿

Accessibility

17 of 28 classrooms are accessible to differently-abled students, along with 2 dedicated accessible restrooms (1 for boys, 1 for girls).

🩺

Health & Nutrition

Periodic medical check-ups and a daily Mid-Day Meal Scheme for all students, with a dedicated kitchen facility.

🚻

Restrooms & Drinking Water

20 restrooms in total (8 for boys, 12 for girls), plus safe drinking water available on campus.

🚗

Road Access

The school is accessible by all-weather roads, connecting it to surrounding villages.
