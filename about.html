Our History

Government Higher Secondary School, Karapattu was established in 1963 and has served students of Karapattu village and the surrounding Uthangarai block of Krishnagiri district for over six decades. The school is a co-educational, Tamil-medium government institution offering education from Class 6 through Class 12 (Higher Secondary), and is administered by the Tamil Nadu School Education Department.
