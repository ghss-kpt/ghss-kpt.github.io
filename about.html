Our History

Government Higher Secondary School, Karapattu — officially a Government Model Higher Secondary School (GMHSS) — was established in 1963 and has served students of Karapattu village and the surrounding Uthangarai block of Krishnagiri district for over six decades. The school is a co-educational, Tamil-medium government institution offering education from Class 6 through Class 12 (Higher Secondary), administered by the Tamil Nadu School Education Department. School UDISE Code: 33310903002.
