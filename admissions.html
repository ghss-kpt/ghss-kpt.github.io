Admissions

Who Can Apply

Admission is open to all children of the appropriate age for Classes 6 through 11, as per Tamil Nadu School Education Department norms. Class 9 and Class 11 admissions also accept transfer students from other recognised schools, subject to seat availability.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the school office during working hours ([Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM] — confirm timing) to collect an admission form, or download it here once uploaded.
  2. Submit the completed form along with the required documents (see below) to the school office.
  3. The school will confirm the seat and share the reporting date.

Documents Required
